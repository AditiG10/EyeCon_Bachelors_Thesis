Our fundamental goal is to allow impaired
paralysed and handicapped patients to convert
their intents which will be gauged using eye blinks and converted to text and speech.



Why EyeCon?


Humans have become heavily relied on the technological appliances around them. In order to interact with the system, users rely on various devices such as the keyboard, mouse etc. With the help of these devices, systems have become so accessible that even novice users don’t struggle while using them.

While most of the users don’t think twice before using these devices, they aren’t generally designed by keeping the specifications required by the patients struggling with motor disabilities in mind. One billion people, or 15% of the world’s population, experience some form of disability. Hence, it is quite essential that alternative technological solutions are made, so that these patients can also be an active part of society.

The natural motion of an eye is the eye blink and it can be used efficiently to communicate information. There are various tools which can be used to detect these eye- blinks however most of these tools are quite expensive & heavy. It is also essential that these alternative solutions are not bulky and easy on the pocket. Most of the devices these days come with built-in webcams/ front cameras. To utilize this ability of patients to communicate with their eyes, we created EyeCon.



How EyeCon works?


In our project we have detected the eye blinks of paralysed/handicapped patient using webcams or mobile front cameras and have converted them to text and speech respectively. The patient locates desired letter/word on our customised keyboard and blinks accordingly. We have inserted a guide on how to use our Eyeblink panel. The final text appears on Display Screen which can be read by attendent. This text can also be heard using a Speech button available above our keyboard. Hence, our system intends to provide users with efficient and effective methods of communication without relying on other individuals and without using bulky, expensive hardware!
